Salon FAQ

Can the bot show clients my work via images?+

Yes — clients can send a reference photo of a hairstyle they want, and the bot will respond. You can also have the bot send your portfolio when clients ask "show me your work."

What about last-minute cancellations?+

Bot handles reschedules and cancellations automatically. You only get pinged for clients who want to dispute your cancellation policy.

Can it match my salon's vibe (formal, friendly, sassy)?+

Yes — tell us your tone in the onboarding form, and we calibrate the AI's voice. Some salons want professional, some want playful with emojis. Your call.

Will it work with my booking software?+

For Mode 3 we can build a custom integration (extra fee). Otherwise the bot just sends confirmed bookings to your WhatsApp — you add them to your calendar manually or we set up a Google Calendar sync.
