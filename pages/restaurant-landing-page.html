Restaurant FAQ

Can the bot take actual orders with item + qty + address?+

Yes — for casual/takeaway/delivery orders the bot captures item, quantity, address, payment method, and timing. You (or your kitchen) get a clean order summary in WhatsApp. For complex orders or special requests, the bot escalates.

What about allergens, spice levels, dietary restrictions?+

We train the bot on your menu's allergen info, spice levels, and vegetarian/halal/etc. flags. Customers can ask "is the karahi gluten-free?" and get a real answer from your menu data.

Can customers reserve a table?+

Yes — bot asks for date, time, party size, and any special requests (birthday, window seat, etc.). You get a reservation request in WhatsApp with full context. You confirm or counter-propose.

Can I update the daily specials / 86'd items?+

Yes — you send the bot a quick message like "86 the lamb tonight" or "today's special: chicken tikka" and it updates immediately. No dashboard required for daily changes.

Does it integrate with POS or delivery platforms?+

For Mode 3, yes — we can build custom integrations with Foodpanda, Careem, or your POS (extra fee). For Mode 1/2, orders come to your WhatsApp and you enter them in your system manually (most restaurants already do this anyway).
