Frequently asked

How fast can I go live?+

48 hours from when you submit the onboarding form. Most of that is waiting for Meta to verify your WhatsApp Business account.

What languages does the bot speak?+

It auto-detects and replies in whatever language the customer types in — English, Roman Urdu, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Spanish, and ~30 more. No config needed.

What if the bot doesn't know the answer?+

It collects the customer's name and question, then connects them to you on WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. You get notified instantly.

Can it handle voice notes and images?+

Yes. Voice notes are transcribed and understood by Gemini. Images are analyzed — perfect for "look at my X, what do you recommend?" queries.

Is my customer data safe?+

All WhatsApp messages are end-to-end encrypted by Meta. Our backend only stores what's needed to reply. Logs auto-delete after 30 days.

Do I need to know anything technical?+

No. We handle the entire setup. You just fill the form, sign into Meta once to verify, and you're done. Ongoing edits to FAQs/prices are done in a simple form.
