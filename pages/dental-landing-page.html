Dental clinic FAQ

Will patients know they're talking to AI?+

That's up to you. Most clinics don't disclose (it's faster and patients don't care). We can introduce it as "AI assistant for [Clinic Name]" if you prefer transparency.

What happens in a dental emergency?+

The bot immediately escalates to your on-call number. You can configure emergency trigger words ("tooth knocked out", "severe pain", "bleeding") that skip the AI and ping you directly.

Can it handle voice notes from older patients?+

Yes — voice notes are understood in Urdu, Hindi, English, Arabic, and 30+ languages. The bot transcribes and replies in the same language.

What if a patient asks about something not in my knowledge base?+

The bot says: "Main aapki details lekar clinic ko inform kar deta hoon" and collects the patient's name + question. You get a WhatsApp message with everything.

Does it integrate with my clinic management software?+

Not yet out of the box. But we can build custom integrations for an extra fee. Most clinics just have the bot send confirmed bookings to their WhatsApp.
