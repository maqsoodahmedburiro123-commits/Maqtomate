Real estate FAQ

Can the bot show buyers my actual listings?+

Yes — feed it your inventory (location, beds, price, sqft, key features) and it shares matching listings when buyers ask. Buyers can also send a property photo to ask "do you have something like this?"

What about serious buyers vs window shoppers?+

The bot asks qualifying questions (budget, timeline, pre-approved financing, etc.) before escalating. You only get pinged for buyers who meet your criteria. Tire kickers get helpful info but don't waste your time.

Can it book viewings on my calendar?+

Yes — for Mode 3, we integrate with Google Calendar. For Mode 1/2, the bot sends you a confirmed viewing request and you add it manually (or we set up a free Google Calendar sync).

What if a buyer wants to negotiate price?+

The bot declines politely and escalates the offer to you. You decide whether to accept, counter, or pass — no AI is making pricing decisions on your behalf.
