# Maqtomate AI — Universal Outreach Scripts

> **5 cold-outreach templates, niche fill-in tables, objection handlers, and country pricing — copy-paste-ready for any business, any country.**

## 0. Ground Rules

- **The first message must reference something specific about the prospect's business.** No "Hi, I help businesses with AI" — that's spam. Reference their Google reviews, an Instagram post, a recent opening, or a specific service they list.
- **Reply within 1 hour during business hours.** Speed = trust. If you can't reply fast, set up an auto-reply.
- **Goal of the first message:** get a 5-minute call or a "send me more info." Never sell in DM #1.
- **Volume target:** 30–40 personalized touches/week → 1–2 closes. Treat outreach like a job, not a vibe.
- **3-Mode pricing** is canonical (see `docs/pricing-canonical.md`). Use the country table in §5 to localize the numbers.

## 1. The 5 Outreach Templates

### 1A. Direct Value (best for warm-ish leads: someone who already follows you, or you have a mutual connection)

> **Hey [Name], saw [specific thing — a Google review, an IG post, their new branch opening]. Quick question — do you guys get a lot of WhatsApp messages after hours asking about [their top service]?**
>
> **I set up an AI bot for [comparable business in same city/niche] that replies in 10 seconds, books appointments, and tells people your exact prices — even at 2 AM. They recovered about 30% of "lost" after-hours leads in the first month.**
>
> **Worth a 5-min call this week? I can show you the live bot, not slides.**

**Why it works:** specific compliment → real problem → concrete proof → low-commit ask.

---

### 1B. Social Proof (best for cold leads who don't know you)

> **Hi [Name] — I work with [2-3 client names in their niche] on their WhatsApp customer service. Most were losing 30-40% of after-hours inquiries to competitors who replied first.**
>
> **I built a bot that replies in their voice, in [their language], 24/7. Replies under 10 seconds. Books appointments. Quoting exact prices from their menu. Handles FAQs.**
>
> **Your [specific service — e.g., "balayage bookings"] would be a great fit. Open to a 5-min demo this week?**

**Why it works:** name-drop proof → quantified pain → specific fit → low-commit ask.

---

### 1C. Voice Note (best for personal-touch in Pakistan/India/UAE — voice notes feel warmer than text)

> **30-second voice note script:**
>
> "Hey [First name], this is [Your name] from Maqtomate AI. Saw your [specific thing — Google listing, IG bio, recent post]. Quick question: how many WhatsApp messages do you get after 9 PM that you only reply to the next morning? Most [dentists/salons/agents] I talk to say 20-30 a week. We set up an AI bot that handles those in 10 seconds — books appointments, quotes prices, all in your voice. Most clients recover the cost of the bot in the first week from the leads they were losing. Worth a quick chat? Send me 'yes' and I'll send you a 2-min video of a live bot in action."

**Send as an actual voice note on WhatsApp, not as text.** Voice notes get 3-4× the reply rate of text in South Asian markets.

---

### 1D. In-Person Pitch (best for walk-ins, networking events, friends-of-friends)

> **"Hey [Name], I do something you might find useful. I build WhatsApp AI bots for [their niche] — like a receptionist who never sleeps and never charges per message.**
>
> **For [their specific business type, e.g., "a clinic like yours"], it usually pays for itself in the first 2 weeks from the appointments you'd otherwise lose to slow replies.**
>
> **I can show you one I built for [comparable business] right now on my phone — want to see? Takes 2 minutes."**

**Why it works:** pull out phone, show live bot → no slides, no decks, just the thing working. Converts 40-50% of in-person intros.

---

### 1E. Referral Engine (best for warm intros)

> **"Hey [Mutual Connection], you know I'm doing WhatsApp AI bots for [niche]. Quick favor — is there anyone in your circle who runs a [niche] business and complains about slow WhatsApp replies? I just took on [number] new clients this month and want to make sure I'm talking to the right people, not cold-calling."**
>
> Then to the referred person:
>
> **"Hey [Name], [Mutual] suggested I reach out. I do WhatsApp AI bots for [niche] — figured since [Mutual] thought of you, you might be losing leads to slow replies. Open to a 5-min demo?"**

**Why it works:** referral carries trust; the ask is small and specific.

## 3. Objection Handlers

### "Patients / clients won't talk to a bot"
> "Honestly, most of them don't even notice. The bot replies in 10 seconds, in their language, with your exact prices. By the time they realize it's AI, they've already gotten their answer. And the ones who really need a human get routed to you — you only get the calls that matter."

### "We already have a receptionist / we already use X"
> "Cool, then your receptionist is probably answering the same 5 questions 30 times a day. Let the bot handle that so your team can focus on the conversations that actually need a human. We're not replacing your team — we're giving them their evenings and weekends back."

### "What if it gives wrong information?"
> "The bot only knows what you tell it. Services, prices, hours, FAQs — it can't make stuff up. And for anything outside that, it says 'let me get my manager' and connects them to you. Worst case is a confused customer, which is the same as today."

### "I don't trust AI"
> "Fair. You don't have to. We can have the bot introduce itself as 'the AI assistant for [Business Name]' so people know upfront. And the dashboard shows every conversation — you can review, edit, and override the bot's replies anytime. You're always in control."

### "It's too expensive"
> "What's your hourly receptionist cost? For most clients, the bot pays for itself in 1-2 recovered leads per month. Then it's pure margin. Want me to show you the ROI math for your specific case?"

### "Can I try it first?"
> "Yes — we have a 14-day money-back guarantee. If it doesn't work for you, full refund, no questions. Setup is Rs 3,500–8,000 depending on the plan. Most people stay because they see the bookings in the first week."

### "I'm not technical"
> "You don't have to be. We set up everything. You just fill a 5-min form and approve the bot's replies. After that, you get a dashboard — that's it. Most of our clients are 1-person shops, not tech companies."

## 4. Closing Scripts

After a 5-min demo where they see the live bot working:

> **"So you've seen it reply in 10 seconds, in Roman Urdu, quote your exact prices, and book a slot. For [their business type], the standard package is Rs [X] setup + Rs [Y]/month. That covers everything — setup, training, hosting, monitoring, updates. Cancel anytime, 14-day money-back guarantee.**
>
> **Want to start this week? I'll send the onboarding form and we'll be live by [date]."**

If they hesitate:
> **"What's the one thing holding you back? Is it the cost, the time, the trust — what would make this a no-brainer for you?"**

If they say "let me think":
> **"Totally fair. What specifically do you want to think about? Sometimes it's easier if I answer the question right now — saves you Googling."**

If they say "I need to talk to my partner / wife / team":
> **"Makes sense. Want me to jump on a quick call with them? I can do a 5-min demo for them too, same thing. Saves them from you trying to explain it."**

**Track in a simple spreadsheet:**

**After 100 touches, you should know:**
- Which template converts best for which niche
- Which niche has the best LTV
- Your average touches-to-close ratio
- Your average time-to-close

## 7. Red Flags to Avoid

- **Don't pitch "AI".** Pitch "your phone rings less, your bookings double, you sleep at night." Nobody buys AI; they buy outcomes.
- **Don't demo without context.** "Look at this cool bot" is weaker than "watch this bot reply to a real customer query in your niche."
- **Don't compete on price.** Compete on speed of setup, quality of bot, and "I answer my own WhatsApp in 1 hour."
- **Don't oversell AI capabilities.** If they ask "can it do X?" and you don't know, say "let me check" — not "yes."
- **Don't ignore the audit trail.** Every "yes" goes in the dashboard, every "no" gets a polite follow-up in 2 weeks.
